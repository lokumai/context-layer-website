How is the authentication handled in the current service layer, and what is the policy for token expiration?

Authentication Flow

The authentication logic is encapsulated within the @auth_service.py. It utilizes a JWT-based strategy where tokens are validated against the internal identity provider.

According to the #auth-policy, token expiration is strictly set to 60 minutes for standard sessions, with a refresh window of 24 hours.

Token Lifespan Regulations

To maintain a high security posture, all session tokens generated by the ElevenLabs-Identity core must adhere to a strict 3600-second TTL (Time-To-Live). Refresh tokens may be granted for an extended 24-hour period upon specific biometric re-validation.
